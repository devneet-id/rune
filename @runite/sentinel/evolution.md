# @evolution — The Evolution of Eidos

Hello, and welcome to the evolution of Rune.

This folder opened because your runite calls `Rune\Ethereal::evolution();`.
*Eidos* is the form behind a thing: here you rebuild Rune in your own form.
What stays is only the **Concept of Paradigm** — Ether, Essence, Entity, Manifest, Phantasm.

## ✦ What lives here
- `<Rune>/` — runes of your own (`Ether.php`, `Essence.php`, `Entity.php`, `Manifest.php`, `Phantasm.php`).
  Make one with `php cast sentinel --runic-create=<Name>`.

An evolution rune never replaces an origin rune (Aether, Chanter, Cipher, …).

## ✦ The possibility obtained
- [x] Building an entirely new working environment.
- [x] Creating a completely different workflow.
- [x] Making your own Rune, either Public (contribute) or Private (local).
